# India CPI Inflation Analysis (2013–2023)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes India’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation trends from 2013 to 2023 using Microsoft Excel. The objective of the analysis is to identify major inflation drivers, understand category-wise price movement, and derive meaningful economic insights from CPI index data published by the Government of India.

The project focuses on analyzing inflation patterns across multiple CPI categories such as Food & Beverages, Fuel & Light, Housing, Health, Education, and Personal Care. Using Excel-based data analysis techniques, trends and growth patterns were identified to understand how inflation evolved over the decade.

---

## About CPI (Consumer Price Index)

The Consumer Price Index (CPI) measures the average change in prices paid by consumers for a basket of goods and services over time.

* CPI is one of the most important indicators used to measure inflation.
* A rise in CPI indicates increasing prices and higher inflation.
* The base CPI value is generally considered as 100.

### Inflation Formula

Inflation Rate (%) = (Current CPI− Previous CPI / Previous CPI) × 100

# Key Insights from the Analysis

## 1. Food & Beverages Was One of the Largest Inflation Contributors

* Food and Beverages CPI increased significantly from approximately **107 to 179** during the analysis period.
* This indicates a major rise in food prices over the decade.
* Food inflation remained one of the strongest contributors to overall CPI growth.

---

## 2. Fuel & Light Experienced Strong Price Growth

* Fuel and Light CPI increased from approximately **100 to 182**.
* The sharp increase reflects rising fuel costs and energy price volatility.
* Fuel inflation likely impacted transportation and household expenses.

---

## 3. Health and Personal Care Showed Consistent Upward Trends

* Health-related CPI categories demonstrated continuous growth over time.
* Personal Care and Effects also showed steady inflation increases.
* These categories indicate rising lifestyle and healthcare expenses in India.

---

## 4. General CPI Index Showed Long-Term Inflation Growth

* The overall General Index steadily increased throughout the decade.
* Inflation growth accelerated during certain periods due to economic and global factors.
* The analysis showed long-term price growth across nearly all categories.

---

## 5. Some Categories Experienced Slower Inflation Growth

* Certain sectors such as Education and Recreation showed relatively stable growth patterns compared to Food and Fuel categories.
* Inflation impact varied significantly across sectors.
